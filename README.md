## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze customer transactions, product performance, customer segments, and payment preferences.

It combines Excel formulas, What-If Analysis, regression, PivotTables, charts, and interactive dashboard elements to generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze customer transaction patterns.
- Identify high-value customers and products.
- Compare customer segments and payment preferences.
- Practice statistical analysis and scenario evaluation.
- Build a dashboard for business reporting and storytelling.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- Date and time functions
- Dynamic filtering using `FILTER`
- What-If Analysis and Goal Seek
- Conditional formatting and growth indicators
- Linear regression analysis
- Product and customer comparisons
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- KPI cards, slicers, and timeline
- Business insights and interactive dashboard

---

## 📚 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 📅 Date & Time Analysis
Used date and time functions to analyze transaction records.

### 2. 🔎 Filtering & What-If Analysis
Practiced `FILTER`, Goal Seek, and scenario-based analysis.

### 3. 📈 Statistical Analysis
Performed linear regression and reviewed regression statistics using the Analysis ToolPak.

### 4. 🛍️ Customer & Product Analysis
Compared customer values, product performance, and frequently purchased products.

### 5. 📊 PivotTables & Charts
Created PivotTables and Bar, Line, and Pie charts to summarize transaction data.

### 6. 📈 Dashboard & Storytelling
Combined KPI cards, customer rankings, product values, customer segments, payment distribution, slicers, and a timeline into one dashboard.
